A big update, built from your reports. Mixtapes comes to macOS and speaks German now, with more languages to come, and the like button no longer crashes the app.

## New

### Platforms and languages
- macOS support: sign-in, Now Playing, media keys, the menu bar and trackpad gestures, as a disk image for Apple silicon and Intel (#18)
- Translations, starting with German. Preferences, General has a Language row (#34)

### Playback
- Volume normalization turns loud songs down to match the rest, like on YouTube, for streams and downloads (#140)
- On a slow connection, playback waits a few seconds for audio instead of stuttering (#153)
- New setting: start a radio from a song played from search or Home, instead of playing the rest of the list (#139)
- New setting, Linux only: lower the volume while another app plays media, like a voice message in a browser (#166)

### Lyrics
- Two new sources, QQ Music and the community made Unison, both with word by word timing (#168)
- Lyrics can come from a music video's subtitles too
- Fix lyrics running early or late from the lyrics menu, per song and source
- Write your own lyrics: plain text, timed lines or word by word timing, or paste TTML

### Library and playlists
- Reorder songs in your playlists: drag the handles in selection mode, one song or all ticked songs at once (#146)
- Adding a song a playlist already holds asks first (#161)
- Remove All Downloads for a playlist or an album, from its menu or its library card (#150)
- Choose your download folder, and bring your downloads along: Preferences, Downloads (#152)
- Search your listening history, and open it from History beside Listen again on Home (#138)
- Recent searches show under the empty search field (#144)
- Sort the library by recent, oldest or name (#159)
- Likes reach Last.fm and ListenBrainz (#155)

### Interface
- Explore has compact pills, with For You and Genres on one line
- Holding a phone sideways puts the cover beside the controls

## Fixes

- Liking a song from the player bar crashed the app (#142)
- Song version lookups filled your YouTube search history (#143)
- Songs started slowly because a change at YouTube sent every one of them down the slower route
- Icons were missing with icon themes other than Adwaita, such as Breeze (#147, #165)
- Dragging a song in the queue one row down did nothing, and no song could reach the end (#147)
- The queue kept scrolling forever when a drag ended outside it (#147)
- The queue jumped to the top when a song was removed (#157)
- Play Next and Add to Queue did not open an empty queue with Open Queue When Playback Starts on (#145)
- Load More under Top Songs jumped to the top of the artist page (#141)
- Albums and singles were only sorted by year for the first few (#160)
- Music videos did not find their song when the song is titled "Original - Romanized", as many Japanese songs are
- Downloaded songs could show as not downloaded on albums (#156)
- The star on albums appeared late, and the artist page's star had no background (#158)
- Scrollbars no longer sit on top of carousels
- Lyrics showed on a dark box when they filled the player view

## Packaging

- `mixtapes-bin` on the AUR: a prebuilt package, so you no longer have to compile (#167)
